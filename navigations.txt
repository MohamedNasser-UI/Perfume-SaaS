⚙️ Settings
And I would make New Sale visually prominent.

Because operationally, your system has two very different user experiences:

Salesperson:

Customer → Build Perfume → Price → Discount → Payment → Done

Owner/Manager:

Dashboard → Procurement → Inventory → Suppliers → Reports → Settings

That separation will make the system much easier to use than trying to make every feature equally prominent.
